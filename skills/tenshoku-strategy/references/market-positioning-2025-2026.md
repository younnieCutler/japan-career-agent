## Foreign Worker Demand Signals

Concrete effects of the plan to attract 1M foreign professionals by 2026:

- **Stronger visa support:** trend toward easing the Highly Skilled Professional (高度専門職) points system
- **Eased Japanese requirements:** firms running global teams increasingly hire below N2
- **More English-environment firms:** English-as-official-language firms (Mercari, SmartNews, LINE, etc.) keep growing
- **Foreign-specialist agents:** VISIONARY CAREER, RGF, doda Global, and other specialized channels expand

### Negotiation leverage for foreign applicants

| Strength | How to use |
|----------|-----------|
| Bilingual (Japanese + native + English) | Appeal a global-bridge role |
| Overseas market experience | Scarce value for firms planning overseas expansion |
| Multicultural team management | Immediate asset for firms increasing foreign hiring |
| Japan-market adaptation done (3+ yrs) | Basis for resolving 定着性 (retention) risk |

## Strategic Timing Advice

### Optimal job-change timing

| Period | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| Jan–Mar | Targets April joining (新年度). New hiring budgets. Most openings |
| Jul–Sep | Targets October joining (下半期). Backfilling H1 leavers |
| Right after bonus (Jun/Dec) | Resigning after receiving the bonus minimizes loss |

### Periods to avoid

| Period | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| Apr–May | Firms focus on onboarding new grads. Mid-career hiring slows |
| Year-end (mid-Dec~) | Corporate holidays. Hard to schedule interviews |
| Right before a project deadline | Risk of damaging your reputation at the current job |

## How to Use This Data in Negotiation

How to use this market data in salary negotiation:

1. **Present market rate:** "Per doda's role-by-role salary survey, the average for a DE with 3–5 years is ¥6.5M."
2. **Appeal demand:** "DE demand is currently 1.5x year-over-year, so my experience holds high market value."
3. **Regional adjustment:** present Tokyo-baseline data, but adjust for regional firms.
4. **Use timing:** "I prepared to align with the new-fiscal-year hiring window, so it's optimal timing for both sides."

**Caution:** Market data is only a reference. The final salary varies greatly by individual ability, company
size, industry, and employment type. Demanding an unrealistic figure on overconfidence in this data backfires.
