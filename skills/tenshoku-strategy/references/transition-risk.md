# Short tenure, career gaps, and truthful transition explanations

Use this reference only when the user asks about short tenure, repeated job changes, a career gap, or how to explain a transition. It is not a rejection model.

## Short tenure

Keep separate:

```text
tenure_length: documented period
transition_count: observed count
reason: user's factual explanation
period_evidence: confirmed work/responsibility/result from that tenure
```

A short tenure can trigger a retention-risk question, especially when repeated. It is not an automatic failure. Do not require three years, use the 9.5-month survey average as a cutoff, or apply a fixed multiplier.

A useful explanation preserves the actual reason and shows what the candidate learned or will verify next. It does not replace the reason with generic `growth`, `challenge`, or `culture fit` language.

## Employment gap

Represent the gap and what happened during it as separate facts:

```text
employment_gap: true | false | unknown
gap_period: confirmed dates or Unknown
gap_activity: study | qualification | care | travel | rest | job_search | other | Unknown
gap_explanation: factual user statement or Unknown
career_relevance: verified | unknown
```

Study or certification activity can be relevant evidence, but it does not turn the period into employment or erase the gap. Do not assign a universal penalty.

## Career values

Compensation, working hours, evaluation, role scope, location, work mode, learning, mission, stability, and other user-confirmed conditions are independent career values. Do not rank salary or benefits as lower-quality motives via a needs hierarchy.

Interview wording should still be relevant and coherent: a true career value may need to be connected to the target conditions without pretending it is a different motive.
