# 年収交渉 (Salary Negotiation) Guide

> Salary negotiation is the process of proving and justifying your market value.
> Japanese companies often decide the offered figure with reference to your previous salary.

---

## Timing Rules

| Interview stage | Can you raise salary? | Note |
|-----------------|-----------------------|------|
| 1st interview | No | Stage for showing competence and motivation. Money talk reads as "low motivation" |
| 2nd interview | Depends | Answer if the interviewer asks first. Do not bring it up yourself |
| Final interview | Yes | Answer when the interviewer or HR asks "希望年収は?" |
| After 内定 (offer) | Optimal timing | After the conditions are presented, you can negotiate politely |

**Principle:** Waiting until the company raises the salary topic is the Japanese manner. However, when going
through an agent (recruitment firm), the agent negotiates on your behalf, so direct mention is unnecessary.

---

## Market Rate Research Checklist

Market data to confirm before negotiating:

- [ ] **doda salary survey** (doda.jp/guide/heikin/) — average salary by role/years
- [ ] **OpenWork** (openwork.jp) — the company's actual salary range (by band)
- [ ] **転職会議** (jobtalk.jp) — per-company salary data + interview experience reports
- [ ] **Recruit Agent data** — confirm directly with your CA
- [ ] **The JD's salary range** — confirm the floor and ceiling

## Japanese Phrase Bank (10 Key Phrases)

### 1. Stating desired salary (basic)

> 「現在の年収は○○○万円です。前職での○○の成果と、御社での○○への貢献可能性を踏まえ、○○○万円程度を希望しております。」

(My current salary is ¥XXXm. Based on my ○○ results at my previous job and my potential to contribute to ○○ at
your company, I am hoping for around ¥XXXm.)

### 2. Asking the company first (humble form)

> 「私の経験とスキルセットを考慮した上で、御社ではどの程度の評価をしていただけるのでしょうか。」

(Considering my experience and skill set, how would your company evaluate me?)

### 3. When the offer is low (request to reconsider)

> 「ご提示いただいた条件に感謝いたします。ただ、私のスキルセットを考慮いただき、もう少し調整の余地があるか、慎重にお伺いしたく存じます。」

(I appreciate the conditions presented. That said, considering my skill set, I would like to carefully ask
whether there is any room for adjustment.)

### 4. Mentioning a competing offer (leverage)

> 「他社様のオファーとの比較よりも、御社の事業ビジョンに大きな魅力を感じております。処遇面でもう少しご配慮いただければ、確信を持って合流したいと考えております。」

(Rather than comparing with other companies' offers, I am strongly drawn to your business vision. With a little
more consideration on compensation, I would join with full conviction.)

### 5. Negotiating other conditions instead of salary

> 「年収面が難しい場合、リモートワークの頻度や、入社後の評価タイミングについてご相談できますでしょうか。」

(If salary is difficult, could we discuss remote-work frequency or the post-join review timing?)

### 6. Acceptance

> 「ありがとうございます。ご提示いただいた条件で、ぜひ入社させていただきたく存じます。」

(Thank you. I would very much like to join under the conditions presented.)

### 7. Requesting review time

> 「大変魅力的なお話をありがとうございます。家族とも相談の上、○日までにお返事させていただいてもよろしいでしょうか。」

(Thank you for the very attractive offer. After consulting with my family, may I reply by the ○th?)

### 8. When asked to confirm current salary

> 「現在の年収は基本給○○○万円、賞与込みで年収○○○万円となっております。」

(My current salary is a base of ¥XXXm, and ¥XXXm including bonus.)

### 9. Responding when told the salary table is the limit

> 「テーブル上の制約があることは承知しております。入社後の最初の評価時期に改めてご相談させていただくことは可能でしょうか。」

(I understand there are table constraints. Could we discuss it again at the first review period after I join?)

### 10. When going via an agent (to the CA)

> 「希望年収は○○○万円ですが、企業側の予算感を確認していただけますか。交渉のポイントがあればアドバイスもお願いいたします。」

(My desired salary is ¥XXXm — could you check the company's budget sense? If there are negotiation points,
please advise.)

## Leverage Signals Table

| Strengthens leverage | Weakens leverage |
|----------------------|------------------|
| Holds a competing offer (even verbal) | Currently unemployed (exposes urgency) |
| Holds a rare tech stack (SRE, ML Ops, etc.) | Holds only generic skills (e.g., plain Web FE) |
| Current salary is already at market | Current salary is far below market (anchor effect) |
| High interview ratings (fast to the final) | Barely passed documents / long waits |
| Can join immediately (引き止めなし) | Resignation timing uncertain (3+ months wait) |
| Japanese N1 + work experience | Japanese N3 or below (communication risk) |
| The company is hiring urgently (急募) | Regular/unhurried hiring |

## Negotiation Decision Tree

```
Company presents salary
    ├── Within desired range → Phrase #6 (accept) or #7 (review time)
    ├── 5–15% below desired
    │   ├── Has competing offer → Phrase #4 (leverage)
    │   ├── No competing offer → Phrase #3 (request reconsideration)
    │   └── Says it's a salary-table limit → Phrase #9 (post-join re-review) + #5 (other conditions)
    └── 20%+ below desired
        ├── Company is highly attractive → Phrase #9 + #5 (staged approach)
        └── Company moderately attractive → politely decline: "ご期待に添えず申し訳ありません"
```

## 報酬用語の評価 (Comp Terminology — compare by total amount)

> Adapted from career-ops (MIT, github/santifer/career-ops): `modes/ja/_shared.md`.
> Japanese postings/negotiations have terms absent from EN markets. **Compare annual total, not just monthly.**

| Term | Meaning | Evaluation impact |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| **正社員** | indefinite employment; covered by social insurance · 有給 · 賞与 · 退職金 | annual = monthly × 12 + 賞与 (usually 2–6 months) |
| **業務委託 (フリーランス)** | contract work, sole proprietor | monthly looks high but no social insurance/賞与/退職金 → compute a 正社員 equivalent |
| **賞与/ボーナス** | twice a year (summer/winter) | a big share of 正社員 comp. annual = monthly × (12 + bonus months). Never omit in comparison |
| **年俸制** | annual total divided by 12 or 14–16 | 賞与 may be included in the 年俸 — always confirm the breakdown |
| **みなし残業/固定残業代** | a fixed amount of overtime pay pre-included in the monthly salary | check whether "〇 hours" is stated + whether excess is paid separately. **Can be a red flag** |
| **36協定** | labor-management overtime agreement | regulates the overtime cap. A "white company" indicator |
| **試用期間** | usually 3–6 months | standard in Japan, not a red flag |
| **退職金** | a lump sum at resignation | common at large/traditional firms, rare at startups. A long-tenure-premised condition |
| **通勤手当** | commuting cost reimbursement | standard in Japan. Confirm the cap (e.g., ¥50k/month) |
| **住宅手当/家賃補助** | housing allowance | some firms ¥20k–100k/month |
| **健康保険/厚生年金** | social insurance (mandatory for 正社員, company pays half) | hidden 正社員 comp. Important when comparing vs 業務委託 |
| **有給休暇** | statutory 10–20 days/year | check the usage rate. Too low = red flag |
| **退職予告** | 正社員 usually 1–2 months' notice | plan the start date around the current job's 退職予告 period (→ `enman-taishoku.md`) |
| **ストックオプション** | startup equity | evaluate vesting, cliff, tax treatment (qualified/non-qualified) |

### 業務委託 vs 正社員 comparison script

> 「公正に比較するため、パッケージの完全な構成を理解したいです：基本給、賞与、有給、社会保険、
> 通勤・住宅手当、退職金。業務委託の場合、これらを考慮した月額換算はいくらになりますか？」

(To compare fairly, I'd like to understand the full package: base, 賞与, 有給, social insurance, commuting/housing
allowance, 退職金. For 業務委託, what is the monthly equivalent accounting for all of these?)

### みなし残業 confirmation script

> 「月給にみなし残業が含まれている場合、何時間分が含まれていて、超過分は別途支払われますか？」

(If みなし残業 is included in the monthly salary, how many hours are included, and is the excess paid separately?)

> **Link:** Use this table together with the オファー面談 ① 給与条件 check in `naitei-taiou.md`. Without a total
> comparison, a high-monthly 業務委託 can be misleading.

## Anti-Fabrication Rule

- Do not inflate current salary. Japanese companies may request a 源泉徴収票 (withholding slip).
- Do not fabricate a non-existent competing offer.
- When claiming "market value," always cite a concrete data source.
