# 入社手続き & 定着90日 (Onboarding Paperwork · Reference Check · First 90 Days)

> The tail of the pipeline: from 退職日 to surviving the 試用期間. Three modules:
> **① 入社手続き paperwork (書類・住民税・社会保険)**, **② 前職調査 / reference check**,
> **③ 定着90日 plan (試用期間 survival)**. 転職 success = 定着, not the first day.

**Core Principle:** Paperwork gaps cost real money (double insurance premiums, tax surprises); the first
90 days decide whether the 早期離職 risk flagged at matching time materializes. Checklist delivery, no
encouragement.

**Not legal/tax advice.** Figures and procedures per 厚労省/国税庁 public guidance — verify current-year
rules for anything money-related.

## 1. 入社手続き Document Checklist

### 1-1. Receive from the OLD employer (退職時 — chase these before leaving)

| Document | Needed for | Timing |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| 雇用保険被保険者証 | New employer's 雇用保険 enrollment | 退職日 or shortly after |
| 源泉徴収票 | New employer's 年末調整 (same-year job change) | ~1 month after 退職 (legally within 1 month) |
| 年金手帳 / 基礎年金番号通知書 | 厚生年金 enrollment (if employer held it) | 退職日 |
| 離職票 (雇用保険被保険者離職票) | **Only if a gap exists** — 失業給付 application | Request explicitly; not automatic when a next job is set |
| 退職証明書 | Sometimes requested by new employer / 国保 switch | On request (employer must issue — 労基法22条) |
| 健康保険資格喪失証明書 | 国保 switch during a gap | On request |

### 1-2. Submit to the NEW employer (typical set)

雇用保険被保険者証 · 源泉徴収票 · 基礎年金番号 · マイナンバー · 給与振込先 · 扶養控除等申告書 ·
健康保険被扶養者異動届 (with dependents) · 入社誓約書 / 身元保証書 (company forms) · 卒業証明書 or
資格証明 (if required). Missing 源泉徴収票 → new employer cannot run 年末調整 → user must file 確定申告.

### 1-3. 住民税 — the classic surprise

住民税 is billed on **last year's** income, one year behind. Resignation timing changes the mechanics:

| Case | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| No gap + 特別徴収の継続手続き done | Old employer files 給与所得者異動届出書 → deduction continues at the new employer. Cleanest. |
| 1月〜5月 退職 | Remaining amount through May is **lump-sum deducted (一括徴収)** from the final pay — the last payslip can be visibly small. Warn in advance. |
| 6月〜12月 退職 + gap | Switch to 普通徴収 → payment slips arrive at home. Budget for them. |

### 1-4. Social insurance during a gap

| Gap | Action |
|-----|--------|
| None (退職翌日 = 入社日, or same-month) | Nothing — pension/health continue via the new employer |
| 1 day+ gap | Choose within 20 days: **任意継続** (old 健保, up to 2 years, full premium self-paid) vs **国民健康保険** (rate depends on last year's income) — compare both premiums, do not default. 国民年金 switch at the 市区町村 within 14 days. |

Even a 1-day gap between 資格喪失 and new enrollment creates a 国民年金/国保 obligation for that month
(month-end rule: insurance for a month is owed where you are enrolled on its **last day**). Month-end 退職日
vs mid-month changes who bills that month — check before agreeing on the 退職日 (→ `enman-taishoku.md`).

## 2. 前職調査 / Reference Check

Standard at 外資系 and increasingly at ハイクラス/管理職 selections; rare-but-existing at 日系.

- **Consent is required** (個人情報保護法) — a check without your knowledge via a screening agency is not
  the normal pattern; you will be asked to nominate referees or sign a consent form.
- **Referee selection:** 2–3 people who can speak to *recent, direct* work — former manager > skip-level >
  peer. Brief them: which role, which achievements will be asked about, timeline.
- **Consistency is the whole game:** titles, tenure, scope, and achievement claims in the 職務経歴書 must
  match what referees say. This is why fabrication is banned upstream (`job-seeker-agent` evidence rules) —
  経歴詐称 discovered here = 内定取消; discovered after joining = 懲戒解雇 grounds.
- Current employer must NOT be contacted before resignation is announced — state this constraint explicitly
  when asked for referees (「現職への連絡は退職意思表明後にお願いします」). Standard and respected.

## 3. 定着90日 — 試用期間 Survival Plan

### 3-1. What 試用期間 legally is

解約権留保付労働契約: dismissal during/at the end of probation is *somewhat* easier for the employer than
normal 解雇 but still requires objective, reasonable grounds — it is not at-will. Typical length 3–6 months.
Conditions during probation must match what the 労働条件通知書 stated (→ `roudou-joken-review.md` §2-⑤).

### 3-2. 30 / 60 / 90 plan (中途 = 即戦力 expectation management)

| Phase | Goal | Concrete actions |
|-------|------|------------------|
| **D1–30: Context** | Map how decisions are made | Learn 社内用語 and decision lines; identify the 3 people your output depends on; **over-communicate via 報連相 early** — in Japan, early over-reporting reads as trustworthy, silence reads as risk; confirm expectations with your manager in week 1 (「最初の3ヶ月で何ができていれば合格ですか」— ties to オファー面談 ③ mission) |
| **D31–60: First win** | One small, visible, completed deliverable | Pick something scoped and finishable over something impressive and half-done; write it down (doc/ticket) so it is legible to non-witnesses |
| **D61–90: 再現性** | Prove the resume's 再現性 claim | Repeat the win pattern once more in a different spot; propose one improvement rooted in fresh-eyes observation (framed as question, not criticism of predecessors) |

### 3-3. Early-mismatch protocol

- If reality diverges from the written offer (role, remote, overtime) → **log it with dates**, raise it
  calmly with the manager first; the 労働条件通知書 is the reference document (→ `roudou-joken-review.md`).
- Do not decide "this was a mistake" inside the first 30 days on atmosphere alone — but a *documented*
  conditions breach is different from an adjustment period. Distinguish the two explicitly for the user.
- If exit becomes rational: a probation-period departure is recoverable in the market **if** the reason is a
  documented conditions breach; frame via `taishoku-riyu-reframing.md`. Do not encourage or discourage —
  present the record and the trade-offs.

## Honesty Gate

- Do not soften the 一括徴収 / double-premium warnings — money surprises break trust in the whole plan.
- Do not coach referees to exaggerate. Briefing = alignment on facts, not scripting praise.
- 30/60/90 items must map to the user's actual role; do not paste the generic table without adaptation.
