# 退職理由 and 転職軸: factual reframing

Reframing reorganizes the user's own facts into a clear explanation. It is not a reason to conceal a
material fact, invent effort, or claim a company's culture.

## Four-part evidence chain

```text
Why leave: user's confirmed reason
What was tried: confirmed action, or Unknown
Why this role/company: dated JD or company fact, or Unknown
Why now: user's timing and constraints, or Unknown
```

If an effort was not made, say so or ask whether the user wants to explain the structural constraint.
Do not write “I improved efficiency”, “I sought growth”, or “I want a stable company” without evidence.

## Draft template

```text
I decided to change roles because [confirmed structural reason].
In my current role, I [confirmed action], and I learned [confirmed result or Unknown].
I am exploring [target role] because the JD states [dated requirement/company fact].
I would like to contribute through [candidate evidence], while confirming [Unknown].
```

The candidate reviews the wording. Personal circumstances, compensation, relationships, workload,
location, and authorization may be stated factually; none must be converted into a stereotype about
the previous employer or target company.

## Deep-dive questions

- What exactly changed, and when did you observe it?
- What did you personally own or try?
- What evidence supports the result or limitation?
- Which part of the target role is confirmed, and which part must be verified?
- What would make the next role a better fit for the user's stated 転職軸?

No numeric metric is required. If the user has a metric, preserve its source and date; if not, use
qualitative wording without estimation.
