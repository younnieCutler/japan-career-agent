# 退職理由 Strategic Reframing Guide

> A strategic conversion table that replaces a resignation reason from "past dissatisfaction" with "future contribution."
> In Japanese business culture, criticizing a former employer raises doubts about organizational fit.

---

## Core Principle

Every answer must end on **future-oriented contribution**.
"I left because I disliked ~" → "I chose a new environment in order to achieve ~"

What the interviewer is checking:
1. Did you **make an effort instead of avoiding** the problem (努力の過程)?
2. Is the motivation **developmental and logical** (前向きな理由)?
3. Will you **leave us for the same reason** (定着性)?

---

## Negative-to-Positive Conversion Table

### 1. Salary / evaluation dissatisfaction (給与・評価への不満)

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Raw reason | Pay is low / I wasn't fairly evaluated |
| Reframed (JP) | 「実績と貢献度を正当に評価していただける環境で、より高い成果を出したいと考えました。」 |
| Reframed (EN) | "I want to produce higher results in an environment that fairly evaluates achievements and contribution." |
| Keywords | meritocracy, transparent evaluation criteria, reward synergy |
| Follow-up prep | "What results specifically?" → always prepare a quantified STAR example |

### 2. Excessive overtime / WLB collapse (残業過多・WLB崩壊)

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Raw reason | Too much overtime / can't rest |
| Reframed (JP) | 「限られた時間の中で最大の成果を出す生産性の高い働き方を実現し、長期的に御社に貢献したいと考えました。」 |
| Reframed (EN) | "I want to realize a highly productive way of working that maximizes results within limited time, and contribute long-term." |
| Keywords | efficiency improvement, focus, long-term contribution |
| Follow-up prep | "What did you do to reduce overtime?" → a work-efficiency attempt is required |

### 3. Relationships / friction with boss (人間関係・上司との不和)

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Raw reason | Bad workplace relationships / doesn't fit with the boss |
| Reframed (JP) | 「チーム単位での協力や活発なコミュニケーションを通じて、より大きな価値を生み出せる環境で力を発揮したいと考えました。」 |
| Reframed (EN) | "I want to apply my strengths in an environment that creates greater value through team-level collaboration and active communication." |
| Keywords | collaborative culture, flat communication, synergy |
| Follow-up prep | "What problem specifically?" → never blame an individual. Generalize to "a difference in communication style" |
| DANGER | This is the most dangerous reason. It is safest not to use the word "人間関係" in the interview at all |

### 4. Monotony of work (業務内容のマンネリ化)

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Raw reason | Same work every day / can't grow |
| Reframed (JP) | 「新しい技術の習得や職務領域の拡大を通じて、専門性をさらに深めたいと考えました。」 |
| Reframed (EN) | "I want to deepen my expertise further through acquiring new skills and expanding my scope of work." |
| Keywords | challenge spirit, self-directed growth, deepening expertise (スキルアップ) |
| Follow-up prep | "What did you try for growth at your current job?" → an effort process (internal transfer request, self-study) is required |

### 5. Company prospects / management instability (会社の将来性・経営不安)

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Raw reason | The company looks risky / the industry itself is declining |
| Reframed (JP) | 「成長ポテンシャルの高い環境で自分のスキルを試し、事業の拡大に貢献したいと考えました。」 |
| Reframed (EN) | "I want to test my skills in a high-growth-potential environment and contribute to business expansion." |
| Keywords | contributing to company growth, market development, stable contribution |
| Follow-up prep | "Why do you think our company has growth potential?" → research on the target company is required |

### 6. Location / commute time (勤務地・通勤時間)

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|
| Raw reason | Commute is too long / I want to return home |
| Reframed (JP) | 「居住地に密着した勤務を通じて、業務への集中度を高め、安定的に貢献したいと考えました。」 |
| Reframed (EN) | "Through work close to my residence, I want to raise my focus and contribute stably." |
| Keywords | time management, local contribution, intent for stable tenure |
| Follow-up prep | This reason is relatively safe. Can be conveyed as-is. But always add "the intent to stay long-term at your company" |

## Creator / Engineer Specific Variants

When a professional uses the reason "I couldn't build the career I wanted":

**Banned pattern:**
> "会社がチャンスをくれなかった" (the company didn't give me a chance) — other-centered thinking

**Required pattern (include 努力の過程):**
> 「現在の組織内で目標達成のために〇〇のような努力を重ねましたが、構造的な環境の制約により実現が難しく、転職を決意しました。」
> ("Within my current organization I repeatedly made efforts like ○○ to achieve the goal, but structural
> environmental constraints made it hard to realize, so I decided to change jobs.")

**Engineer examples:**
- Proposed an internal tech study group, ran a PoC for new-tech adoption → but it couldn't be adopted under a legacy-first policy
- Wanted to join upstream phases (requirements, basic design) → but the org structure assigned coding only

**Creator examples:**
- Proposed an internal project to strengthen the portfolio → but was assigned only client revision work
- Proposed adopting a design system → but the policy maintained existing templates

## Foreign Applicant Variants

When there is a visa-related reason:

**Safe phrasing:**
> 「ビザの切り替えに伴い、より専門性を活かせるポジションを探しています。」
> ("Along with a visa change, I am looking for a position where I can better leverage my expertise.")

**Caution:** Do not directly express anxiety about visa status. Focus on "leveraging expertise" rather than
"stability of residence status."

---

## Anti-Fabrication Rule

Reframing is **tactical restructuring**, not **lying**.

- If the logic collapses under the interviewer's deep-dive (深掘り質問), trust is lost instantly
- Always confirm that the core content of the converted answer is fact-based
- If "the effort process" did not actually happen, do not invent it → switch to a different reason instead
