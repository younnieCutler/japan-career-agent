# 円満退職 (Graceful Resignation) — Evidence Checklist

This is a communication and handover checklist, not legal advice and not a universal notice-period rule.
The applicable answer depends on the employment contract, work rules, employment term, dispatch arrangement,
collective agreement, and current official guidance. Do not present a generic period as a confirmed fact.

## Before communicating a resignation

Record the evidence before choosing a date:

- contract type, fixed-term end date, and employer of record;
- the relevant work rules or internal resignation procedure;
- the intended last working day and the new employer's confirmed start-date constraint;
- handover scope, owners, open risks, and company property to return;
- any visa, health-insurance, tax, or benefit question that needs an official answer.

If an external legal or market claim is needed, record its URL, publisher, publication date, observation date,
confidence, and expiry in `_shared/career_claims.yml`. If it is not confirmed, report `Unknown` and ask the user
to verify it with the employer, an official authority, or a qualified professional.

## Communication sequence

1. Ask the user's direct manager for a private conversation unless the contract or a documented safety concern
   requires another route.
2. State the decision briefly, using a factual reason connected to the user's own plans. Do not invent a reason
   or disclose confidential information.
3. Confirm the required written notice and the employer's acceptance/processing steps from the work rules.
4. Agree on handover deliverables in writing. Keep the user's copy of the plan and dated messages.
5. Contact HR or the dispatch agency when the contract requires it. Do not assume that the new employer or an
   agent can change the current employer's procedure.

Suggested opening:

> お時間をいただきありがとうございます。今後のキャリアについて検討した結果、退職の意思を固めました。
> 必要な手続きと引き継ぎについて、ご相談させてください。

## Counter-offer and pressure

Present the offer, promised change, or request as an `Observed` event. Do not decide for the user. Ask:

- Which confirmed condition would actually change the user's decision?
- Is the proposed change written, owned, and dated?
- Does it address the user's confirmed must-have or only offer a temporary concession?
- What remains unknown about implementation?

The user's choice and any override should be recorded separately from the evidence.

## Handover record

| Item | Evidence to record |
|---|---|
| Deliverables | owner, status, location, acceptance condition |
| Recurring work | next due date and successor |
| Risks | known issue, impact, mitigation owner |
| Access and assets | return/revocation instruction from the employer |
| Final documents | document name, requester, confirmed delivery date |

Use the actual dates from the pipeline. Labels such as `T-14` are planning placeholders, not legal deadlines.

## Safety and escalation

If the user reports harassment, retaliation, withheld documents, immigration risk, or a disputed contractual
condition, pause generic coaching. Preserve evidence and recommend an appropriate official or professional
consultation. The assistant must not make a definitive legal conclusion from missing documents.
