# 労働条件通知書: written-condition review

Review the written conditions before the user accepts or signs. This is a comparison and question
workflow, not legal advice. Cite the current official government source for any legal statement and
ask qualified advice for a dispute.

## Evidence boundary

Compare three records separately:

1. written offer / 労働条件通知書;
2. what the employer or CA said, with date;
3. the user's current written employment conditions, if the user chooses to provide them.

No verbal promise is treated as confirmed until it appears in the written document or a dated written
confirmation. Missing text is `Unknown`, not acceptance.

Use the comparison labels `Improved`, `Equivalent`, `Downgrade`, or `Unknown` only when both source
documents support the comparison. Do not treat a missing current-job figure as a downgrade or a pass.
Do not use a total compensation number to hide a downgrade in base pay, hours, location, or scope.

## Contradictions

```text
Written item: [quote]
Earlier statement: [quote, source/date]
State: Contradictory
Question: Could you confirm which condition governs and provide the corrected term in writing?
```

If the route is through an agent, the user may ask the CA to relay the question. The assistant never
contacts the employer.

## User-owned conclusion

```text
Confirmed downgrades: [items]
Unknown before acceptance: [items]
Questions to resolve: [items]
Trade-offs: [what changes if the user accepts]
Decision: user chooses after reviewing the evidence
```

Never say a user must accept or reject. Preserve the documents and the user's decision as separate
records.
