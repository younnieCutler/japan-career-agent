# 面接後フォロー + お礼メール (Post-Interview Follow-up)

> Follow-up contact between the interview and the result. **Japanese job-change is mediated by the agent**, so
> unlike US-style active chasing (a mail every 7 days), keep it **light**. The お礼メール is a supplementary
> tool (do not over-rely on it) — what wins is the interview content, not the thank-you mail.
>
> Adapted from career-ops (MIT, github/santifer/career-ops): `modes/followup.md`.
> Japan-localized: keigo template, via-agent branch, light cadence.

**Core Principle:** Value first, chasing is a minus. Polite and concise. Do not fabricate facts.

---

## 1. お礼メール — whether / when to send

| Situation | Recommended |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Direct application (no agent)** | Once, same-day (当日中, before leaving on interview day). A supplementary plus |
| **Via agent (CA)** | **Send feedback/thanks to the CA first.** A direct mail to the company is usually unnecessary (the CA mediates). Only when you were given a direct contact |
| **After a final / 役員 interview** | A single polite mail same-day is recommended |
| After a カジュアル面談 | Optional. If sent, keep it short |

> sincereed `XZQrE796ANo`/`73aB6xl_Dbs` tone: "There are things you can do after the interview, but don't
> over-rely." The core is the interview content + 自己分析; the お礼メール only marginally polishes the impression.

### お礼メール template (same-day, keigo)

```
件名: 本日の面接のお礼（氏名）

〇〇株式会社 〇〇部 〇〇様
本日はお忙しい中、貴重なお時間をいただき誠にありがとうございました。
〔a specific point discussed in the interview〕について伺い、〔connect your experience/再現性 in one sentence〕
という点で貢献できると改めて感じました。
引き続きご縁をいただけましたら幸いです。何卒よろしくお願い申し上げます。
氏名 / 連絡先
```

**Rules:**
- Mention **one real point that actually came up** in the interview (avoid a generic thank-you).
- One sentence of value (貢献) + connect a real achievement (reuse `shokumukeireki-saigensei.md` 再現性). Do not
  fabricate a non-existent result.
- ~250 chars, once. No pressure/chasing like "ご検討よろしく".
- Voice: natural Japanese, no literal-translation style, avoid exaggerated adverbs ("圧倒的に" etc.).

## 2. Follow-up cadence (Japan — light)

Unlike US 7-day chasing, in Japan the agent mediates and selection takes longer, so **chasing backfires**. Defaults:

| Situation | First follow-up | After | Note |
|-----------|----------------|-------|------|
| 書類選考 no response | **Confirm via the CA after 1–2 weeks** | CA's call | Do not pressure the company directly |
| Result delayed after interview | Ask the CA for progress | — | If a direct application, one polite inquiry |
| 回答期限 approaching (offer side) | → `naitei-taiou.md` 回答期限 negotiation | — | |

**Over-chasing warning:** repeated chasing at short intervals reads as "impatient / self-centered" → a minus.
Point this out directly.

---

## 3. Via-agent (CA) branch

- Follow-up / お礼 / progress checks all go **to the CA first**. The CA relays appropriately to the company.
- Right after the interview, share **honest self-feedback** with the CA (what went well / what worries you) →
  the CA uses it to reinforce the company recommendation and prep the next round.
- When using multiple agents, no duplicate contact with the same company (オーナーシップ, `job-seeker-agent`
  application-route rule).

---

## Honesty Gate
- Do not fabricate, in the お礼メール, a point that did not come up in the interview or a non-existent achievement.
- Do not use a non-existent other-company process as follow-up-mail leverage (→ same principle as
  `nenshu-koushou.md` / `naitei-taiou.md`).
- Response language follows the SKILL.md Language Auto-Detection rule. Domain terms (お礼メール/書類選考/内定)
  stay in original Japanese.
