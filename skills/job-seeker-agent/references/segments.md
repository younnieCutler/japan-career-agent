# 中途 segments — content adjustments, not decision rules

Segments change the questions and evidence bar, never the canonical decision contract. Do not use
age or segment labels as a hidden penalty and do not infer a platform route without dated evidence.

## dai2_shinsotsu

Ask about the first role, the reason for change, work actually performed, and transferable evidence.
Treat student-era episodes as supplementary. Coordinate with `tenshoku-strategy` for 退職理由, but
never invent a motive.

## standard

Ask for scope, decisions, methods, outcomes, and repeatability. Years alone are not evidence of a
requirement. Missing evidence remains `Unknown`.

## senior_ic

Ask for domain depth, cross-team scope, technical or business decisions, and the evidence that the
candidate can reproduce the method in the target context. Do not turn age into a requirement.

## management

Ask for organizational scope, hiring/development decisions, budget or delivery responsibility, and
evidence of coaching. A title alone does not demonstrate management capability.

## Route and interview handling

Platform choice belongs to `references/platforms.md` and must cite the claim registry or a current
observation. Interview round guidance is a question hypothesis unless the company describes its
process. Reference checks, language, authorization, and gaps are handled as explicit facts or
unknowns, never as an unverified rate or stereotype.
