# 職務経歴書: From Duties to Reproducible Evidence

This is a writing reference, not a recruiter scoring rule. It reorganizes facts the candidate supplied;
it never adds a result, metric, role, or skill.

## 履歴書 and 職務経歴書 roles

Treat the documents as overlapping but different surfaces, not as `facts` versus `story`.

- 履歴書: concise candidate profile — identity, education/work history, qualifications, and other
  requested profile fields; it may also contain 志望動機 or appeal points.
- 職務経歴書: detailed evidence of work experience, practical capability, responsibilities,
  achievements, transferable knowledge, and alignment with the employer's needs; it may also contain
  志望動機 and 自己PR.

Follow the employer's requested format when one exists. Do not imply a single statutory template.

## Four evidence axes

For each bullet, ask:

| Axis | Question |
|---|---|
| Role | What was the candidate's scope, distinct from the team's? |
| Choice | What did the candidate decide or change, and why? |
| Result | What changed? Keep a metric only when confirmed. |
| Reproducibility | What method could the candidate explain and defend again? |

If role or choice is unknown, mark the bullet as a duty and ask a follow-up. Do not upgrade it with
adjectives. If the result is unknown, use qualitative wording such as scope, frequency, or observed
change only when the user confirms it. An approximate number is not a substitute for a missing metric.

## Progressive readability

Structure the document so a reader can understand the role and evidence quickly while the same claims
also survive detailed review. Use headings, short sentences, selective bullets, and visible scope.
Do not optimize around a universal `30-second` reading claim.

## Summary template

```text
[years/domain, if confirmed] + [one supported capability] +
[representative action/result] + [target-role connection]
```

Every phrase must trace to a resume line or user statement. The target-role connection comes from the
JD; it is not proof that the company will select the candidate.

## AI-assisted source text

AI-generated or AI-edited resume text is untrusted draft material. Validate every factual assertion
against confirmed evidence and remove anything the candidate cannot defend. Do not use AI-detector
scores or detector-evasion language as a quality gate.

## Evidence review output

```text
Confirmed facts used: [source lines]
Unknowns requiring a question: [items]
Draft changes: [fact-preserving edits]
Claims removed: [unsupported metrics or skills]
```

Use this reference with `evaluation_rules.md`, which labels requirements `Matched`, `Missing`, or
`Unknown`. Do not use a numeric rating, coefficient, private recruiter rule, or outcome estimate.
