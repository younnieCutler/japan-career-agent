# 志望動機・転職軸・面接深掘り (Motivation, Job-Change Axis, Interview Probing)

Strict rules for building **志望動機 (motivation)**, defining the **転職軸 (job-change axis)**,
and surviving the **面接の深掘り (interview probing)** in Japanese mid-career (中途) selection.

> This is content/answer strategy (面接の **内容**). Interview **manner** (入室/服装/退室) lives in
> `tenshoku-strategy/references/mensetsu-manner.md`. 退職理由 reframing lives in
> `tenshoku-strategy/references/taishoku-riyu-reframing.md` — pull the reframed 退職理由 from there and
> keep it logically consistent with the 志望動機 produced here (see §3, 4-WHY chain).

**Anti-fabrication (suite rule):** every element below must trace to real experience or real research
the user provides. Reframing is tactical reordering of true facts — never invented facts. If the user
cannot supply the 自分の経験 or 会社理解 a section requires, leave it blank and say so; do not write it for them.

---

## 1. 志望動機 — The Forced 3-Part Structure

A passing 志望動機 is not "why this industry sounds nice." It must connect the **company** to the
**candidate's own track record** to a **concrete post-join contribution**. Enforce this order every time:

```
① 会社理解 (Company understanding)
   — A specific, researched reason this company (not its competitors). Strategy, business model,
     position, culture — cite something only true of THIS company.
② 自分の経験 (Your own experience)
   — A real past experience/skill that maps directly to what ① requires. This is the bridge.
③ 入社後貢献 (Post-join contribution)
   — What you will concretely do for them with ②, framed as a Giver (see §4).
```

Output template:

```
■ 会社理解:  「御社の〇〇という戦略／事業に注目しています。理由は△△だからです。」
■ 自分の経験: 「前職で□□を担当し、◇◇という成果を出しました。」
■ 入社後貢献: 「この経験を活かし、入社後は〇〇の領域で△△に貢献したいと考えています。」
```

**Why all three are mandatory:** ① alone = "anyone could say this." ②+③ without ① = "you could go
anywhere." Only the chain proves *this candidate × this company* fit, which is what the interviewer scores.

> ②ストーリーになっていない / ③Takerになっている" as the three NG patterns, plus a chapter

### 1-1. 志望動機 NG patterns (auto-flag these)

| NG pattern | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **志望動機になっていない** | Describes the industry/role generally, never *this* company | Add ① with a company-specific fact from research |
| **ストーリーになっていない** | ①②③ are disconnected bullet points, no causal thread | Make ② the literal reason ③ is credible and ① is attractive |
| **Taker になっている** | Only states what the candidate gains (年収/成長/環境) | Re-anchor on ③ 入社後貢献 — what the company gains (see §4) |
| **逆質問が貢献に繋がらない** | Reverse questions sound like demands, not interest | See §5 |

## 2. 転職軸 (Job-Change Axis)

The 転職軸 is the candidate's **single consistent criterion** for choosing roles — it must reconcile the
退職理由 (push) and the 志望動機 (pull) into one logic. Interviewers use it to test 定着性 (will this
person leave us for the same reason?) and decision maturity.

**Build the 転職軸 in this order (collect interactively, 2–3 questions at a time):**
1. What must the next role have that the current one structurally cannot give? (the axis)
2. Is that need permanent or a passing frustration? (maturity check)
3. Does the target company actually satisfy the axis? (consistency check — if not, the 志望動機 will collapse)

**Good 転職軸 traits:** future-oriented, role/skill-based (not person/money-grievance-based), and true
of the target company. **NG 転職軸 traits:** vague ("成長したい" with no definition), money-only,
or contradicted by the target company's reality.

> Evidence basis: video `4v07J2u3i7Y`「転職軸はどう答える？」chapters: "転職軸とは？ /
> 企業が転職軸で知りたいことは？ / 転職軸の良い回答は？ / 転職軸のダメな回答例は？". Detailed
> phrasing 확인 불가; structure above is reconstructed from chapter topics + general 中途 norms.

## 3. The 4-WHY Consistency Chain (面接の深掘り対策)

Japanese interviewers probe (深掘り) until the story breaks. The four WHYs must form **one chain that
does not contradict itself**. Run this consistency check before any interview-prep output.

```
なぜ転職? (Why leave)      → from 退職理由 (tenshoku-strategy/taishoku-riyu-reframing.md)
   ↓ must lead naturally into
なぜこの会社? (Why us)     → from 志望動機 ① 会社理解 (§1)
   ↓ must be supported by
なぜこの職種? (Why this role) → from 転職軸 (§2) + 自分の経験 (§1②)
   ↓ must justify timing
なぜ今? (Why now)          → market timing / readiness; must not contradict なぜ転職
```

**Consistency failures to flag (these are where 深掘り breaks people):**
- 退職理由 says "wanted more X" but 志望動機 target company has *less* X → contradiction.
- 転職軸 is "specialization" but 志望動機 is "broad generalist role" → contradiction.
- なぜ今 is "career timing" but 退職理由 is "couldn't stand it anymore" → reveals the real (negative) reason.

When you detect a contradiction, **state it plainly and fix the weakest link** — do not paper over it,
because the interviewer's 深掘り will find the same gap.

### 3-1. 併願 (other applications) handling

When asked "他社も受けていますか / 志望順位は": stay consistent with the 転職軸. State that other
applications **share the same axis** (proves the axis is real, not a whim), and that this company best
satisfies it. Never claim "only your company" if untrue — it reads as dishonest and breaks 深掘り.
(Evidence: chapters of `uo-UcnSDeys` — "なぜ併願企業を聞くのか / 志望順位の答え方".)

## 4. Give-and-Take: avoid the Taker frame

Interviewers down-score candidates whose motivation is purely what they receive (年収・成長・環境・WLB).
Re-anchor every motivation on a **Giver** frame: lead with contribution, let the gains be implicit.

| Taker phrasing (NG) | Giver re-frame (OK) |
|---|---|
| 「成長できる環境だから」 | 「〇〇の経験を活かして△△に貢献し、その過程で専門性をさらに深めたい」 |
| 「年収を上げたいから」 | (move 年収 to オファー面談 / 年収交渉 — not 志望動機) |
| 「働き方が良いから」 | 「集中して成果を出せる環境で、□□の成果を最大化したい」 |

> Evidence basis: `VzB8ECUBE7Q` chapter "③Takerになっている" listed as a志望動機 NG; the same video
> tags "Give and Take" in its title metadata. 年収/WLB belongs in offer-stage, not motivation — see
> `tenshoku-strategy/references/naitei-taiou.md` and `nenshu-koushou.md`.

## 5. 求人票 reading with Will-Can-Must (企業研究 → 志望動機 fuel)

Strong 会社理解 (§1①) comes from reading the 求人票 correctly, not from the company's slogans.
Map each role to **Will / Can / Must**, and note that **歓迎条件 (welcome) is not 必須 (must)** — treating
welcome items as blockers makes candidates self-reject from viable roles.

| Lens | Question | Use in 志望動機 |
|---|---|---|
| **Will** (会社の意志) | What is this company *trying to become*? (strategy, new business) | Powers ① 会社理解 |
| **Can** (自分が出来る) | Which 必須条件 do I already meet? | Powers ② 自分の経験 |
| **Must** (会社が求める) | What outcome does the role exist to produce? | Powers ③ 入社後貢献 |

The overlap of all three is the sharpest 志望動機. (Evidence: video `WZtGa-imLF4`
「企業研究のコツ…Will・Can・Mustで求人票を読み解く」, chapters incl. "歓迎条件はMustではない？".)
