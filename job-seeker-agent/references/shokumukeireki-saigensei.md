# 職務経歴書 — From 担当業務 to 再現性 (Reproducibility Writing Rules)

How to make a 職務経歴書 / 自己PR show **再現性 (reproducibility)**, **成果 (results)**,
**役割 (role)**, and **工夫 (the deliberate choices you made)** — instead of a flat list of
**担当業務 (duties assigned)**. This is the writing-rule companion to the *scoring* rules in
`references/evaluation_rules.md`.

> **再現性 (Reproducibility)** is the central concept Japanese mid-career screening uses: "If we hire this
> person, can they reproduce this result *here*?" A duty list answers "what were you assigned." A
> reproducible entry answers "what did *you* decide and do that *you could do again for us*." Score and
> rewrite toward the second.

**Anti-fabrication (suite rule):** these rules reorganize and surface *existing* facts. They never add
results, numbers, or roles the user did not actually have. Where a metric is genuinely unknown, use the
"estimate-OK vs never-fabricate" rules in `SKILL.md` STEP 4 — qualitative-but-specific beats invented numbers.

---

## 1. The core transform: 担当業務 → 再現性・成果・役割・工夫

For every bullet the user gives, rewrite along four axes. A bullet that shows only the first axis is a
duty; a bullet that shows all four proves reproducibility.

| Axis | Question it answers | Duty-list bullet (weak) | Reproducible bullet (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **役割 (Role)** | What was *your* scope vs the team's? | "ECサイトの運用を担当" | "5名チームで在庫データ整合性の担当として…" |
| **工夫 (Deliberate choice)** | What did *you* decide to do differently / why? | "データを集計した" | "手集計のミスが多かったためSQLで自動チェックを設計し…" |
| **成果 (Result)** | What changed because of the 工夫? | "業務を効率化した" | "確認工数を週X時間削減し、月△件あったミスをほぼ0に" |
| **再現性 (Reproducibility)** | *How* did you do it (transferable method)? | (absent) | "原因を分解→仮説→検証の手順で進めた（他業務にも再現可能）" |

**Rule:** if a bullet cannot fill **工夫** and **役割**, it is a duty, not an achievement — mark it and
ask the user a follow-up to recover the choice they made. Do not promote a duty to an achievement by
adding adjectives ("しっかり運用した").

> Evidence basis (verifiable chapter structure; transcript detail 확인 불가): video `Mr22EBLr0x0`
> 「評価される自己PRと避けるべきNG3選」chapters explicitly list the failure modes
> "①ズレた強みをアピール / ②成果とプロセスがセットになっていない / ③成功要因の深掘りができていない",
> and a chapter "再現性が判断されるポイントは？". The four-axis transform operationalizes
> "成果とプロセスをセットにする" + "成功要因を深掘りする".

## 2. 職務要約 (Career Summary) — the 3–5 line opener that gets read

Screeners read the 職務要約 first and often decide there. Required content, in order:
1. Years + domain + the **one** capability you are known for (the spine of your 再現性).
2. The single most representative 成果 (with its 工夫 in one clause).
3. The forward hook → connects to the target role (do **not** restate the whole history).

```
■ 職務要約 (template)
「〇年間、△△業界で□□に従事。特に〔再現性のある強み〕を強みとし、〔代表的な工夫〕により
 〔代表的な成果〕を実現。現在は〔ターゲット役割〕で〔貢献〕を目指しています。」
```

(Evidence: video `l4OtUvtsh4o`「大企業の書類選考を突破する職務経歴書の書き方」chapters:
"職務要約 / 職務経歴 / スキル・資格 / 自己PR / 大企業での注意点 / ツール・AIを活用する際の注意点".)

## 3. 自己PR — three NG patterns to auto-flag

| NG pattern | Detection | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **ズレた強み (mismatched strength)** | The strength is real but irrelevant to the target role's Must | Re-pick the strength from §1 that maps to the JD's core requirement |
| **成果とプロセスが分離** | States a result with no 工夫, or a process with no 成果 | Pair them in one sentence (工夫 → 成果) |
| **成功要因の深掘り不足** | "うまくいった" without *why* it worked | Add the transferable reason → this is the 再現性 the interviewer scores |

The same three are the most common reasons a strong-sounding 自己PR still gets a low 再現性 read.
(Evidence: `Mr22EBLr0x0` chapters above.)

## 4. 履歴書 vs 職務経歴書 (don't confuse the two)

- **履歴書**: facts/format document. Screeners *deduct* for errors here (写真, 空白, 誤字, 本人希望欄 misuse),
  they rarely *add* points. Keep it clean and conventional.
- **職務経歴書**: the *evaluation* document where §1–§3 reproducibility writing wins points.

Put energy where points are *earned* (職務経歴書), but never lose free points to a sloppy 履歴書.
(Evidence: video `7l67KHpYlek`「書類選考で落ちる履歴書のNG4選」chapters: "履歴書でチェックされること /
履歴書作成の注意点 / オススメフォーマット / 本人希望欄は書いたほうがいい？".)

## 5. AI-assisted writing caution (state this to the user)

The channel repeatedly flags AI-generated 職務経歴書 / 自己PR as a risk (`l4OtUvtsh4o` chapter
"ツール・AIを活用する際の注意点"; `RqLAixaZyfE` adaptive-test chapter "AIを使わない"). Apply:
- AI may restructure *the user's real content*; it must not invent 成果/役割.
- An AI-polished entry the candidate cannot defend in 深掘り is a liability, not an asset — every
  bullet must survive "具体的には？ / なぜそうしたのですか？".

## 7. Recruiter-Side Lens: bullet formula, 6-second gate, risk map

> Adapted from career-ops (MIT, github/santifer/career-ops): `modes/heuristics/recruiter-side.md`.
> Complements §1's 四-axis (役割/工夫/成果/再現性) with the recruiter's read-side: clarity in 6 seconds,
> one bullet formula, and a pre-write doubt map.

### 7-1. Business-value bullet formula

Prefer this shape for every achievement bullet (it forces 再現性 to surface):

```
Action + system/scope + tool/approach + outcome + proof
```

Good patterns:
- `Resolved [problem] in [system], improving [business/system effect].`
- `Built [capability] with [tools], enabling [user/team outcome].`
- `Migrated [old] to [new], reducing [risk/cost/latency].`
- `Improved [metric] from [before] to [after] by [technical action].`

Avoid weak openers when stronger ownership is true: 「担当」「対応」「helped」「assisted」「responsible for」
「worked on」「participated in」. (These hide 役割 and 工夫 — exactly what §1 needs surfaced.)

### 7-2. Six-Second Clarity Gate (職務要約 top-third)

A screener spends ~6 seconds on the top third before deciding to keep reading. The 職務要約 + first
職務経歴 entry must make the target fit **impossible to miss**:
- target role / 強み (the 再現性 spine)
- strongest matching stack or domain
- one production/business outcome
- (only when relevant) location/remote/visa fit

If a screener must infer fit from scattered bullets, the 職務要約 fails the gate — rewrite it (§2).

### 7-3. Pre-write risk map (internal — do not print unless asked)

Before rewriting a 職務経歴書 / 自己PR / 志望動機, build a small internal map of the recruiter's likely
doubts, and close each with **real** evidence (never invent evidence to close a doubt):

| 想定される疑問 | CV/経歴の根拠 | 候補者向けの打ち手 |
|---|---|---|
| このスタックを実務でできるか | 一致するツール・システム・案件 | 正確なスタックを文脈付きで明示 |
| 十分にシニアか | オーナーシップ・スコープ・トレードオフ・育成 | 在籍年数でなくシニア行動で示す |
| ドメインは関連するか | 類似ユーザー・業務・規模・制約 | 隣接実績を JD の言葉に翻訳 |
| ロジ面のブロッカーは | 勤務地・年収・在留資格・時期 | 聞かれた箇所だけ正直に |
| 応募が汎用的すぎないか | 弱い/広いだけの箇条書き | この役割の具体課題に寄せて書き直す |

This map feeds §1 (which duty-bullets to rewrite first) and `shibo-doki.md` (which doubt the 志望動機 must
preempt). Use it to reduce review risk — not to manufacture claims.
