# 内定対応 (Offer Handling) Guide

> Tactics from the moment you receive an 内定 until joining is confirmed. This is the bridge between
> 年収交渉 (→ `nenshu-koushou.md`) and 円満退職 (→ `enman-taishoku.md`). Covered here:
> **オファー面談 checklist · 内定辞退 contact · 回答期限 negotiation · 入社日 negotiation**.
>
> Adapted from career-ops (MIT, github/santifer/career-ops): `modes/oferta.md` Block C (level strategy).

**Core Principle:** An 内定 is not the finish line — it is a verify-and-align window. Understand (理解) the
conditions precisely, decline politely and quickly, and adjust start date / response deadline without losing
leverage. No encouragement; deliver protocol.

## 1. オファー面談 Confirmation Checklist (5+1)

An オファー面談 (offer meeting) is by default a place to **understand (理解) conditions, not to negotiate**.
Because it comes after the pass is confirmed, check all 5 items with a "confirming" attitude. Missing them
leads straight to post-join mismatch and regret.

| # | Item | What to ask specifically |
|---|------|---------------------------|
| ① | **Understand 給与条件** | base/bonus/みなし残業 inclusion, salary structure, first review timing |
| ② | **評価制度 · 昇格基準** | which outcomes map how to evaluation/promotion |
| ③ | **入社後ミッション and later キャリアパス** | the mission right after joining + expected role in 1–3 years |
| ④ | **Actual 働き方** | remote frequency, real overtime, travel/work location |
| ⑤ | **入社時期 adjustability** | whether you can secure handover time at the current job (→ §4) |
| ＋ | **就業場所 change possibility (extra)** | transfer / work-location change risk |

**Principle:** Can you do 年収交渉 at the offer meeting? → Yes, but first *understand* ① precisely, then
proceed. Branch the negotiation script to `nenshu-koushou.md`. If via an agent, the CA handles schedule and
negotiation (→ §5).

## 2. 内定辞退 (Declining an Offer) — polite + fast

When you must decline another company's offer (multiple offers, or a change of direction). Key: **fast +
grateful + clear (no room for reversal)**. A late 辞退 harms the company's hiring schedule → reputation risk
(you may meet again in Japan's small industry).

### Timing
- The moment you decide. By the 回答期限 at the latest. If "still considering but mostly declining," notifying
  early is good manners.
- Prefer **phone first → mail to supplement**. Mail-only is informal.

### 辞退 script (phone / verbal)
> 「先日は内定のご連絡をいただき、誠にありがとうございました。大変ありがたいお話ですが、
> 慎重に検討した結果、今回は辞退させていただきたくご連絡いたしました。貴重なお時間をいただいたにも
> 関わらず、申し訳ございません。」

### 辞退 mail template
```
件名: 内定辞退のご連絡（氏名）

〇〇株式会社 採用ご担当 〇〇様
お世話になっております。〇〇です。
このたびは内定のご連絡をいただき、誠にありがとうございました。
慎重に検討を重ねました結果、誠に勝手ながら今回は内定を辞退させていただきたく存じます。
選考にあたり多くのお時間を頂戴したにも関わらず、このようなご連絡となり深くお詫び申し上げます。
末筆ながら、貴社の益々のご発展をお祈り申し上げます。
```

### Rules
- **Keep the reason concise.** If pressed, politely use "他社のご縁" or similar. No badmouthing of the previous
  company or other firms.
- **Leave no room for reversal.** "I might reach out again" is NG.
- If the offer came via an agent, **tell the CA first** and have the CA relay it to the company.

## 3. 回答期限 (Response Deadline) negotiation

To compare multiple processes by **aligning offer timing (揃える)**, adjusting the response deadline is key.
An unreasonable extension worsens the impression → do it politely, once.

### Script
> 「大変魅力的なお話をありがとうございます。人生の大きな決断ですので、家族とも相談の上、
> 〇月〇日まで（=内定日+約1週間、通常1〜2週間）お返事させていただけますでしょうか。」

| Situation | Recommended |
|-----------|-------------|
| Other process ends within 1 week | Request that date + a few days as the deadline (give concrete grounds) |
| Other process is 3+ weeks away | Hard to make every company wait → reset priority / a realistic deadline |
| Unemployed + single offer | Weak leverage → consider fast acceptance over an extension |

**Principle:** Do not directly say "I'm comparing other companies, please wait" (use the "家族と相談" frame).
A long extension without grounds can be misread as a decline.

## 4. 入社日 (Start Date) negotiation — careless requests are 命取り

Mishandling the start date can endanger the 内定 itself. Adjust based on the current job's **就業規則 notice
period** and **handover**, in a "as soon as possible, but without strain" tone.

- **法律 vs 就業規則:** legally 2 weeks (正社員), but in practice respecting the 就業規則 (1–3 months) is the key
  to 円満退職 (→ `enman-taishoku.md`). When negotiating, cite "就業規則上〇ヶ月前の申し出が必要".
- **Cases where negotiation is easy:** not 急募 / aligned with a quarter or year start / a clear handover rationale.
- **NG:** vaguely "as late as possible"; a long delay without grounds → read as low motivation / a hard negotiation.

### Script
> 「現職の就業規則上、退職の〇ヶ月前申し出と引き継ぎが必要なため、〇月〇日入社を希望いたします。
> 可能な範囲で前倒しできるよう、現職とも調整いたします。」

## 5. When going via an agent (CA)

- Response deadline / start date / 年収 are **negotiated by the CA on your behalf**. Consult the CA before
  contacting the company directly.
- 内定辞退 too is usually via the CA. Share the honest reason with the CA (they relay a polite version).
- When using multiple agents, beware オーナーシップ (duplicate application) conflicts — you cannot apply to the
  same company via two routes (see `job-seeker-agent` application-route rules / channel `ACUtdXmJ1_g`「オーナーシップ問題」).

## 6. Structural Problem Flag (point out directly)

- Unemployed + single offer but demanding a long 回答期限 → warn: **no leverage, offer-rescind risk**.
- Trying to make all other companies wait via a long extension → warn: **impression worsens at every company**.
- Vaguely trying to delay the start date → warn: **motivation doubted → 内定 wobbles**.
- Delaying a 辞退 → sooner = minimal reputational/relationship loss. Delay only grows the risk.

## 7. 等級/グレード Positioning (Level Strategy — sell senior without lying)

> Adapted from career-ops (MIT, github/santifer/career-ops): `modes/oferta.md` Block C.

- **Level read:** compare the grade (グレード/役職) the JD requires vs the candidate's real level for that role.
- **"Position as senior without lying":** prove senior behavior via **ownership · scope · trade-off judgment ·
  mentoring**, not tenure (`job-seeker-agent/references/shokumukeireki-saigensei.md` 再現性). Never fabricate a
  title or achievement that does not exist.
- **Handling a down-level offer:** acceptable if comp is fair + **negotiate a 6-month re-review and explicit
  promotion criteria in writing** (「初回評価時期に等級の見直しを相談させてください」). Ties into the offer meeting
  (STEP 3-2 ② 評価·昇格基準).
- Keep this positioning consistent across 二次/最終 interviews (`job-seeker-agent` mensetsu-rounds
  `genba-manager`/`exec-final`).

## Anti-Fabrication Rule
- Do not fabricate a non-existent other-company offer as 回答期限/negotiation leverage (→ same principle as `nenshu-koushou.md`).
- Do not fabricate false circumstances as a 辞退 reason. A concise truth + politeness is enough.
- Senior positioning is **reframing**, not lying. Do not invent a title, achievement, or grade you do not have.
