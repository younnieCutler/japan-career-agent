# 面接マナー Protocol

> The full process of a Japanese company interview, from 入室 (entering) to 退室 (leaving).
> An interview is not a competence check — it is a ritual to verify "a working adult we want to work with."

---

## Time Management

| Timing | Action |
|--------|--------|
| 10–15 min before | Arrive at the building. Final outfit check in the lobby/restroom |
| 5–10 min before | Tell reception your purpose: "○時に面接のお約束をいただいております、○○と申します" |
| Interview start time | Wait to be called in the waiting room |

**If you'll be late:**
The **moment** lateness is certain, call immediately (mail is not acceptable):
> 「本日○時に面接のお約束をいただいております○○です。大変申し訳ございませんが、○○の事情により○分ほど遅れる見込みです。」

## Grooming Checklist (清潔感)

清潔感 (cleanliness) is the key keyword of Japanese interviews.

### Common
- [ ] Suit: black / navy / dark gray
- [ ] Shirt: white by default
- [ ] Shoes: black or brown, cleanly polished
- [ ] Bag: a self-standing business bag that holds A4 documents
- [ ] Nails: neatly trimmed
- [ ] Fragrance: none, or minimal
- [ ] Accessories: minimal (a watch at most)

### Men (additional)
- [ ] Tie: a plain pattern (stripe, small dots)
- [ ] Hair: groomed so forehead and ears are visible
- [ ] Facial hair: cleanly shaved

### Women (additional)
- [ ] Makeup: natural tone
- [ ] Hair: if long, tie it so the face is clearly visible
- [ ] Stockings: skin-tone (carry one spare pair)
- [ ] Heels: about 3–5 cm

## 入室 Protocol (7 Steps)

```
1. Knock 3 times (2 is a restroom knock)
2. When you hear "どうぞ" inside → enter saying "失礼いたします"
3. Closing the door: turn back to it and close quietly (do not close behind your back)
4. At the door, give a light bow toward the interviewer (30°)
5. Move beside the chair (the left side of the chair by default)
6. Standing beside the chair: "○○と申します。本日はよろしくお願いいたします。" + a deep bow (45°)
7. At the interviewer's "お掛けください" → sit saying "失礼いたします"
```

**Note:** Never sit before the interviewer tells you to.

---

## 着席 Protocol

- Sit deep in the chair but do not lean on the backrest
- Keep your back straight and chin tucked
- **Men:** feet shoulder-width apart, both hands on your knees
- **Women:** knees together, hands folded on your knees
- Stand the bag on the floor beside the chair (not on your lap / not behind the backrest)
- If you have a coat, fold it on top of the bag

---

## 退室 Protocol (5 Steps)

```
1. At the end → while seated, "本日はお忙しい中、貴重なお時間をいただきありがとうございました。" + bow
2. Stand and bow once more beside the chair (45°)
3. Move toward the door
4. At the door, face the interviewer with "失礼いたします" + bow (30°)
5. Quietly open the door, exit, and close it
```

**Stay composed until you leave the building:** you may run into employees at the elevator, lobby, or
entrance. Do not put on your coat or take out your phone until you have fully left the building.

## Online Interview Variant (Zoom / Teams / Google Meet)

| Item | Protocol |
|------|----------|
| Environment | Background: plain wall or a neutral virtual background. Lighting: keep the face bright |
| Camera | At eye level. Raise the laptop or use an external camera |
| Audio | Earphones/headset recommended. Test beforehand |
| Joining | Join 5 min early and wait. Avoid "まだ入っていませんが…" |
| Greeting | Greet looking at the camera (the lens, not the on-screen person) |
| Screen share | Only when asked. Tidy desktop/notifications beforehand |
| Leaving | Wait until the interviewer leaves first. Or say "失礼いたします" and exit after a 3-second pause |

## Common Mistakes (Severity Table)

| Mistake | Severity | Impact |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Late (no contact) | 致命的 | Interview may be canceled |
| Badmouthing a former employer | 致命的 | Character doubt → immediate reject |
| No keigo / casual speech | 致命的 | Doubts basic working-adult competence |
| 2 knocks | 要注意 | Read as a "restroom knock." A deduction |
| Sitting before the interviewer | 要注意 | Read as lack of manners |
| Crossing legs | 要注意 | Arrogant impression |
| Bag on lap | 軽微 | Read as low manner literacy |
| Entering with coat on | 軽微 | Should be removed when entering the building |

## 面接の地雷ワード (Verbal Landmines — NG remarks that lower the evaluation)

Separate from manner (movement), these are **words/phrases that crater the evaluation if used carelessly**.
Common to in-person and online. The 7 below are items explicitly covered as "地雷ワード" by the channel
(verifiable from public chapters; detailed reasoning unverifiable). The right column is interpretation based on
general Japanese interview norms.

| 地雷ワード | Why it's dangerous (interpretation) | Replacement framing |
|-----------|--------------------------------------|---------------------|
| ①「ワークライフバランス」 (as a motivation) | Taker impression — reads as prioritizing rest over work | Anchor motivation on 貢献; confirm 働き方 at the オファー面談 |
| ②「経営から降りてきて」 | Passive/other-driven — signals not thinking for yourself | Express agency with "自分で課題を捉えて〜" |
| ③「客・業者・あの子」 etc. (forms of address) | Disrespect of customers/partners → character & keigo doubt | "お客様・お取引先・〇〇さん" |
| ④「環境が整っていない」 (about a former job) | Shifting blame / complaining → fear of the same complaint after joining | Express as a structural constraint, including the 努力 process (→ `taishoku-riyu-reframing.md`) |
| ⑤「自社の仕事はAIに代替される」 | Disparaging your own role/industry → motivation & understanding doubt | Reframe to using AI to raise value |
| ⑥「社長・部長」 etc. (titles for own-company people) | Honorifics for your own execs outside the company → immature business keigo | Externally, "弊社の〇〇 (name, no honorific)" |
| ⑦「ぶっちゃけ」 etc. (colloquial) | Basic-competence & TPO doubt | Use a polite form ("率直に申し上げますと") |

> Evidence basis: video `5AFoDOT-4Ws`「面接でこの一言が命取り！…地雷ワード7選」chapters explicitly list these 7
> items with numbers. Each word's detailed explanation (transcript) is unverifiable → the reasons/replacements on
> the right are interpretation based on general norms.

**Rule:** While rehearsing interview answers, auto-scan for these 7 patterns and immediately offer a replacement
framing if found. ④ in particular ties directly to 退職理由, so check it together with the conversion table in
`taishoku-riyu-reframing.md`.
